# Reading list entries and book reviews

For `content/reading.md` (see it for the current entries) and any book review.

- One to four sentences. Lead with the overall take, then what worked and what didn't, then who it's for: "If you're
  looking for an adventure book, this isn't for you."
- Explain the rating when it's mixed: "Rated as a 4 for the interesting ideas and unique concept for a book. Could use
  more in-depth character development."
- Call out tropes and lazy writing directly (stereotyped characters, gratuitous detail) while staying kind to the
  author.
- Entries use `Synopsis:` and `Opinion:` sub-bullets, or a single "Title - one-sentence synopsis" line.
- Write an `Opinion:` only from what Andrew has actually expressed (his GoodReads review, the existing entry, or what he
  tells you). Otherwise write the synopsis and leave `TODO(andrew): opinion`.
