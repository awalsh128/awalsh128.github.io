"|" -}} {{- $token := index $parts 0 -}} {{- $label := cond (gt (len $parts) 1) (index $parts 1) $token -}} {{- $fields = $fields | append (dict "token" $token "label" $label) -}} {{- end -}}

Fill in your own values and the post updates to match.
